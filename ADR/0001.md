🔒 Architecture Decision Record (ADR-0001)

Title: Platform Identity

Decision: The system will be designed as a Universal AI Automation Platform. Social Media Automation is the first business module built on top of the platform, not the platform itself.

Reason:

Multiple business domains (hotel, healthcare, CRM, HR, support) share the same architectural foundation.
Core components such as Identity, AI Runtime, Workflow Engine, Knowledge, Memory, and Integrations are reusable.
New verticals should be added by configuration and modules, not by redesigning the core.
