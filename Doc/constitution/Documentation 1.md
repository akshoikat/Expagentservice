1. Project Name (Working)
Social AI Platform

2. Vision Statement
The Social AI Platform is a production-grade, multi-tenant AI automation platform designed to unify customer communication, AI agents, workflow automation, knowledge management, and business operations into a single extensible system.

The platform enables organizations to connect multiple communication channels such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Telegram, Gmail, and future integrations through a centralized inbox powered by intelligent AI agents.

Rather than functioning as a simple chatbot or workflow builder, the platform serves as an AI Operating System where businesses can create role-specific AI agents, automate complex business processes, manage long-term memory, integrate external services, and monitor all interactions from one unified environment.

The architecture is designed to be modular, scalable, event-driven, secure, and cloud-ready, allowing new integrations, AI capabilities, and business domains to be added without requiring major architectural changes.

The long-term vision is to establish a universal AI automation platform capable of supporting customer service, social media management, hotel reception, healthcare assistance, appointment scheduling, CRM automation, HR operations, sales assistance, and other vertical AI applications from a common foundation.

3. Vision Summary
Build one AI platform capable of powering every business communication and automation workflow through intelligent agents.
